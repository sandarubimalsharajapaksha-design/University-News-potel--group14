News Submission Guidelines

  • Submit event announcements at least 3 days prior to the event date.
  • Include your valid Student Number (SC number) or Staff ID for verification.
  • Attach high-resolution images (JPG/PNG) when submitting event coverage.
  • All submissions are reviewed by the Editorial Board before publication.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How long does it take for submitted news to be published?

Urgent academic notices are published within 4 hours. General club and sports news takes up to 24 hours after verification.

Can student societies request a photographer for an event?

Yes! Select "General Editorial Inquiry" in the form above and mention your event date, time, and venue at least 5 days in advance.

How do I report an error in a published article?

Send the article title and correction details to newsportal@university.ac.lk with the subject line "Correction Request".
